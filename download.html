How to install

  1. Download the installer for your computer.
  2. Run it. On Windows it installs and starts by itself. On Mac, open the disk image and drag the app into Applications.
  3. Sign in to play games, or open Studio to create one.
Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

Click More info, then Run anyway. This appears for apps that aren't code-signed yet.

Mac says Triancle can't be opened

In Applications, right-click (or Control-click) Triancle, choose Open, then Open again. If it says the app is damaged, open Terminal and run xattr -cr /Applications/Triancle.app (or /Applications/Triancle Studio.app).

What still works in the browser?

You can still sign in, change your avatar, shop, and see friends and profiles on the website. Starting a game needs the Triancle app; making games needs Studio.

Where are my Studio games saved?

In this first version Studio saves games on your computer. Cloud saving and publishing to Triancle are coming in a later update.
