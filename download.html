How to install

  1. Download the installer for your computer.
  2. Run it. On Windows it installs and starts by itself. On Mac, open the disk image and drag Triangle into Applications.
  3. Sign in with your Triangle account and press Play on any game.
Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

Click More info, then Run anyway. This appears for apps that aren't code-signed yet.

Mac says Triangle can't be opened

In Applications, right-click (or Control-click) Triangle, choose Open, then Open again. If it says the app is damaged, open Terminal and run xattr -cr /Applications/Triangle.app.

What still works in the browser?

You can still sign in, change your avatar, shop, and see friends and profiles on the website. Starting a game needs the app.
